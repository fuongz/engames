# Reference and design

Reference: user-supplied `knife-game.mp4` (35 seconds). Observed: a rotating round target, knives thrown upward, embedded knives to avoid, remaining-knife counter, three hearts, stage progression, occasional orange/plate boss targets and a shattering target on completion. No code or media from the reference is included.

This version uses an original moonlit citrus orchard, fifteen stages and a three-minute run limit. Every fifth stage is an orange target. Misses spend a shared life and keep the remaining knife count; a cleared stage awards 50 points (150 for special targets), each hit 10 points.

Modes: solo; same-device cooperative play with A / L or left / right touch regions; server-authoritative QR co-op for up to two humans via the existing portal lobby. Partners share a target, three lives and a score. Simultaneous throws can collide, so timing together matters. A disconnected player stops throwing; the other can continue. One human may start a room, following the portal's co-op convention. Late joiners spectate until the next match. Solo runs report scores once on completion; local two-player runs do not enter the solo board. Online scores come from the room.

Cosmetics only: both use the same 60 ms flight, 70 ms cooldown and collision angle. Classic has a short wind trail and four small impact sparks. Settled blades keep only 22% energy to preserve target readability. Lightning wakes are capped at six and impact seals at four; the physical flight remains 60 ms. Glow comes from layered strokes, without animated blur or image allocations. Hits wobble for 200 ms; misses bounce and tumble for at most 700 ms. Effects come from confirmed simulation events (including throw/skin/impact location), so online play never runs client rules. Particles are capped at 160, rings at 12, falling blades at eight; no blur or per-frame image allocations. Reduced-motion mode keeps essential flight and small fading hit marks while removing lightning, rings, wobble, tumbling and screen shake. The preference is read live without extra global listeners.

Classic is free. Storm Dragon costs 2,000 shared tickets in the in-game workshop (B). Selecting a locked dragon opens its shop card rather than equipping it. The shop confirms the price, debits once, and persists ownership and equipment to the account. A signed join ticket carries server-confirmed entitlements into co-op; unauthorized dragon inputs fall back to Classic. Purchases happen before joining an online room. Same-device partners currently use the selected shared skin for new throws. Previously embedded knives retain their own skin. New skins can extend the cosmetic catalog/effect parameters without changing hitboxes or score.

## Generated impact sprite sheet

Built-in ImageGen created `img/storm-impact-sheet.webp`, a 1024×1024 atlas with sixteen 256×256 cells. The original PNG is kept under ignored `packages/games/art-src/phi-dao/storm-impact-sheet-source.png`. A slower (2× duration) GIF preview lives alongside it as `storm-impact-preview-2x-slow.gif`. The PNG's original square size was uniformly resampled to a multiple of four and encoded losslessly, retaining its alpha; frames are never trimmed independently, so the world pivot stays fixed. Inspecting alpha composited over the orchard's dark teal is more informative than raw RGB in near-transparent pixels.

Playback is row-major over 400 ms; the same fixed-origin atlas is used for each confirmed impact. At most four impact animations overlap. The blade, ownership check, flight timing and cooldown stay independent. Missing/failed textures retain the procedural effect, and reduced motion skips this animation. Final in-game appearance and mobile performance await the user's manual test.
